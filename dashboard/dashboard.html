3-Minute Setup Instructions

  1. Open your Google Sheet (e.g. Time_Tracking) in your browser.
  2. Click Extensions > Apps Script in the top menu of your Google Sheet.
  3. Delete any existing code in the editor, click the button below to copy the script, and paste it into Apps Script:
  4. Click the blue Deploy button (top right) > New deployment.
  5. Click the gear icon next to Select type > choose Web app.
  6. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
  7. Click Deploy, authorize permissions when prompted by Google, and copy the Web app URL.
  8. Paste the URL into Google Apps Script Web App URL above and click Test Connection!
